## Design rules

- Keep the same colors and type as the product, so the deck feels like part of it.
- Use real screenshots, cropped tight. Never use mockups that look better than the real product.
- Charts get one takeaway title ("Cut planning time from 2 h to 9 min"), not "Results".
- Put a source line under every external number.

## Live demo protocol (in-person judging)

- **Demo-ready state:** seeded data loaded, logged in, the right tabs open in order, browser zoom 150%, notifications off, laptop charging, display mirroring tested on the venue's screen if possible.
- **Fallback ladder** (rehearse each step):
  1. Live.
  2. Retry once if it's a known transient error.
  3. Switch to a local or offline mode, labeled as such.
  4. Play the backup video clip, saved on the desktop.
  5. Walk through screenshots.

  Say which mode you're in. A calm, honest recovery reads well. Pretending it worked does not.
- **Network:** have a phone hotspot ready; never depend on venue Wi-Fi for the core loop.
- **Rehearse three times with a stopwatch.** Finish 10–15 s under the limit. Decide who answers which kind of question.
- **Science-fair / expo judging:** prepare a 30-second version, a 2-minute version, and a one-screen "start here" state. Judges arrive mid-sentence.
